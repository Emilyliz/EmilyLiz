# Hi, I'm Emily Saavedra 👋

Peruvian economist working at the intersection of **data science and public policy** — across the full policy cycle, from designing interventions and programs to implementation, evaluation, and scale-up. Over the past five years, I've designed and evaluated social programs and led household surveys, RCTs, and impact evaluations reaching 10,000+ people across Peru, Argentina, and Panama, in partnership with institutions including the **IDB**, the **World Bank**, **Duke University**, the **University of Michigan**, Peru's **Central Reserve Bank (BCRP)**, the **Ministries of Housing and Women**, and Peruvian consulting firms.

I'm currently applying to graduate programs in **data science for public policy**, to deepen this work and push evidence-based policymaking further in Peru and Latin America.

🌐 More about my work: **[emilyliz.github.io](https://emilyliz.github.io)**

---

### What I work on

- 🧭 **Program & intervention design** — designing behavioral and social interventions (e.g., video-based treatments, community workshops, leader trainings), informed by fieldwork, focus groups, and qualitative analysis to pilot and refine them
- 🛰️ **Implementation** — sampling design and fieldwork coordination, with quality monitoring to supervise protocols and data collection
- 🧪 **Evaluation** — RCTs and quasi-experimental impact evaluations
- 📈 **Scale-up** — turning validated findings into policy recommendations and programs ready to scale
